Gokul Dhanapati

PhD student in Entomology, Mississippi State University. Research at the Center for Pollinator Health.

I study honey bee health, host–pathogen interactions and colony survival. My work looks at how viral infections affect bee health, immunity and colony survival, with the goal of identifying the factors behind colony losses. Through laboratory research, molecular techniques and field work, I aim to support healthier and more resilient honey bee populations.
