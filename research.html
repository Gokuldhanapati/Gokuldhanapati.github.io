Research

From butterflies to honey bee viruses: my research experience, newest first.

Jan 2025 – present

PhD research: honey bee viruses and colony health

My PhD research focuses on the relationship between honey bee health, viral infections and the environmental stressors that contribute to colony decline. In particular, I study how viruses interact with their honey bee hosts and how these interactions influence colony survival over time.

The overall goal is to identify the key biological and environmental factors associated with colony loss, with a focus on viral dynamics and immune responses.

What I do

  • Honey bee colony management and field sampling of adult bees and eggs.
  • Design and execution of injection-based bioassays for virus–host interaction studies.
  • Virus purification, RNA extraction, cDNA synthesis and qPCR-based molecular analyses.
  • Support for honey bee health extension and outreach activities.
  • Collaboration with multiple laboratories.

2020 – 2022

Master's research: coccinellid beetles

Thesis: Studies on some common coccinellids with special reference to morpho-taxonomy and bioecology of Micraspis spp.

I studied the biodiversity of coccinellids, analysed the genitalia structures and polymorphism of common species, and investigated the seasonality of Micraspis spp. in rice-based cropping systems. I also assisted with insect collection, sample processing and general laboratory work.

Publications

  • Exploring the diversity of coccinellid species: polymorphism and species-specific male genitalia identification
  • An annotated checklist of Coccinellidae (Coleoptera) from Pundibari region of West Bengal, India
  • Morpho-molecular characterization and bioecology of leaf folder, Pycnarmon cribrata (Fabricius), on Nirgundi (Vitex negundo)
  • Biodiversity of insect pest complex of Muskdana, Abelmoschus moschatus (L.), in northern tracts of West Bengal
  • Seasonality of predaceous ladybird beetles Micraspis spp. in rice-based cropping systems of the Terai region of West Bengal
Advisor: Dr. Suprakash Pal, Professor, Uttar Banga Krishi Viswavidyalaya. suprakash@ubkv.ac.in
